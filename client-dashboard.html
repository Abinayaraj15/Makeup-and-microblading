Aftercare Instructions & Checklist

Follow your daily protocol for crisp healed pigment retention.

Today's Care 50%
Day 7 Flaking Notice: Let peeling flakes shed naturally. Picking removes implanted pigment.
  • Days 1–2 (Lymphatic Blot): Gently blot excess lymph fluid every 2 hours on Day 1 using damp sterile cotton. Wash lightly morning and night.
  • Days 3–7 (Thin Balm Layer): Apply half a rice-grain size of healing balm twice daily. Brows may appear darker and begin light flaking around Day 5.
  • Days 8–14 (Ghost Phase & Settling): Pigment temporarily softens beneath regenerating skin before blooming back evenly by Week 4–6.
  • Days 1–3 (Hydration & Straw): Drink cool beverages through a straw and keep lips coated with sterile botanical ointment every 1–2 hours.
  • Days 4–7 (Gentle Shedding): Lips will lightly peel—never bite or peel dry skin. Avoid spicy, acidic, or very hot foods until shedding completes.
  • Days 8–10 (Color Bloom): Tone softens by 30–40% before stabilizing into a natural velvety tint; apply SPF lip balm outdoors.
Essential Do's
  • Pat treated area dry gently
  • Sleep on a clean silk/cotton pillow
  • Apply SPF 50+ once fully healed
Strict Don'ts (10 Days)
  • No picking or scratching scabs
  • No sauna, pools, or heavy sweat
  • No retinol, AHA/BHA, or brow makeup
