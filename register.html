VELOURA Studio Privacy Policy
1. Client Confidentiality & Health Records

At VELOURA Permanent Makeup & Brow Studio, we safeguard client medical history, allergy disclosures, and skin evaluation records with strict medical-grade encryption. Information submitted via our booking and consultation forms is strictly used for treatment qualification and artist preparation.

2. Photographic Documentation & Consent

Before-and-after procedural photographs are taken for clinical documentation. Images are only published or featured in our portfolio gallery with explicit, written client consent.

3. Communications & Data Rights

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third parties. You may request data deletion or update your communication preferences at any time by contacting concierge@velourastudio.com.
